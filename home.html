Barber Spot

Barber Spot is a mobile platform that helps clients discover barbers and barbershops, browse services, book appointments, communicate with their barber, and manage reservations.

For barbers and barbershops

Manage services, availability, client appointments, and business profiles in one place. Eligible users can connect a YouTube channel to stream live video using the Barber Spot LIVE feature.

For clients

Find barbers, book available appointments, receive booking updates, and contact professionals through the app.

YouTube connection

Barber Spot LIVE requests YouTube authorization only when a barber chooses to connect their YouTube account. The barber can disconnect their YouTube account from the LIVE settings. Livestreams are subject to YouTube's policies and availability.
