Barber Spot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Barber Spot handles information when you use our mobile application for barber appointments, profiles, messages, and marketplace listings.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we process account details (such as name, email address, telephone number, and account identifiers), barber and barbershop profiles, appointment and service information, transaction records, messages, reviews, photos and videos you upload, marketplace listings, and support requests. Location information may be used to find nearby barbers and barbershops. Our technology providers may process device identifiers, notification tokens, and technical diagnostic data needed to operate and protect the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts; display profiles and nearby businesses; schedule, manage, cancel, and notify users about appointments; process payments and refunds; provide messaging and marketplace features; deliver notifications; maintain security; troubleshoot errors; provide support; and comply with legal obligations.

3. Payments

Barber Spot uses third-party payment services, including Stripe, to support payment processing. Payment providers may collect and process payment details under their own privacy policies. Barber Spot may receive transaction identifiers, amounts, statuses, and related records needed to operate the service. We do not claim to store full payment card numbers.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with the barber, client, or barbershop involved in a booking or interaction; with other users where you publish a public profile, review, or marketplace listing; and with providers supporting authentication, hosting, payments, and push notifications, including Supabase, Cloudflare, Stripe, and OneSignal as applicable. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service. We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Location, Photos, and Notifications

Location-related features help you find nearby services. Photos and videos you choose to upload may be displayed to other users according to the feature used. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

6. Data Security and Retention

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No system is perfectly secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect users. Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Where available, you may update account information within the app. To request assistance or account deletion, email barberspot.support@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

8. Children's Privacy

Barber Spot is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International Processing

Service providers may process data in locations other than your state or country, subject to applicable safeguards and law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services or legal requirements. We will post the revised policy with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, support, or deletion requests, email barberspot.support@gmail.com.
