Barber Spot — Account and Data Deletion

Account deletion instructions for the Barber Spot mobile app.

Barber Spot users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data using the steps below. This page applies to Barber Spot clients, barbers, and shop owners.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Barber Spot app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your profile or account settings.
  3. Choose Delete Profile (Eliminar perfil) and follow the on-screen confirmation steps.

Request deletion without using the app

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to barberspot.support@gmail.com with the subject Barber Spot Account Deletion Request. Include the email address associated with your account and state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted. Do not send passwords or payment card details. We may need to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Data deleted and data that may be retained

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated personal information, such as profile details and uploaded profile content, subject to technical dependencies and applicable retention obligations. Some records, such as payment and transaction records, records needed for tax or accounting obligations, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and dispute resolution, may need to be retained for the period required by applicable law or legitimate obligations. Data held independently by payment processors may be subject to their own retention policies.

If you need details about what was deleted or retained in your particular case, contact the support email above. We will explain any applicable retention requirement and period where available.
